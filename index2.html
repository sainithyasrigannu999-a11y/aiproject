About the institute

Where ambition meets opportunity.

Sumathi Reddy Institute of Technology for Women is committed to nurturing future-ready women leaders, innovators and professionals through academic excellence and holistic development.

Vision

To become a centre of academic excellence and innovation that empowers women to lead with confidence and integrity.

Mission

To provide quality education, practical exposure and an inclusive learning environment rooted in values and impact.

Core Values

Excellence, integrity, inclusivity, innovation and leadership in service of society.

We combine a modern academic framework with industry-relevant learning, mentorship, research culture and student support systems that help every learner flourish.
